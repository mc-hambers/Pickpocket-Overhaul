# 0.1.26 perk caps and bump rules

## Implemented

Adamant enables managed maximums and locks the Absolute maximum chance slider and native setter. HandtoHand.esp local perk 00001B (MAG_MisdirectionPerk) unlocks the high tier. Local installed record inspection verified its description: maximum pickpocket chance 100%. The vanilla/Adamant Misdirection record 058201 instead grants equipped-weapon stealing and is deliberately not used for this cap. No plugin records or companion mods are edited.

Normal maximum is 89 without the cap perk, 99 with it. Only an eligible first unrecognized bump in the current one-game-hour cooldown can raise that by 1, to 90 or 100. The perk does not guarantee that chance: skill, awareness, weight, value, witnesses, failures and stolen-stack pressure still apply. Managed absolute limits also constrain extra karma-protected successes. Without Simonrim, the existing editable maximum remains. Main Ordinator and Vokrii documented perks continue through the existing native perk entry point; their published perk lists do not identify an equivalent perk-gated maximum. No speculative form IDs or blanket cap locks are added for them. Requiem's broader calculation/skill redesign and optional third-party chance replacements are not certified compatible.

Bumps do not affect NPCs with current Sneak or Pickpocket >=75. Observers who actually see the player make contact become immune to further bump benefits for one in-game hour. Direct target recognition uses the preceding native-monitor sample, avoiding the automatic contact turn counting as prior recognition. Recognized direct bumps cannot earn the extra percentage point. Subsequent distinct bumps clear the active extra point; ordinary repeat-contact suspicion rules remain. Small-to-large callbacks for one collision retain that collision's bonus. A genuine failed theft blocks all bump benefits against that victim for one game day, including canceling an active bonus. These per-NPC game-time deadlines are serialized separately in the SKSE co-save, preserving all existing records.

## Current witness behavior

Witness candidates are living loaded actors in the victim's cell, within 700 units of the victim; followers and sleepers are excluded. At most 64 remaining nearby candidates are considered. A witness must be within 900 units of the player, have native LOS to the player and victim, and have a positive native detection level for the player. Facing the player increases attention but is not a mandatory gate for crime witnesses. Guild witnesses count only for friends/allies of the victim; mapped outlaws count only for their mapped faction. These exemptions differ from personal bump observers.

A qualifying witness contributes clamp(attention*0.12 + (guard?4:0),0,12). Aggregate contribution is capped at 45 before the Witness strength multiplier. At default strength 1 and Awareness strength 50, the aggregate can contribute up to 22.5 percentage points to cap loss and separately to execution loss. Final chance takes the lower capped/execution result; those two losses should not simply be added. The displayed witness debug suffix measures cap without witnesses minus actual cap, including clamping and existing penalty multipliers.

Nearby guards/alerted NPCs also contribute separate personal awareness (6 each, up to 18), even before witness visibility/exemption checks. Nearby actors can likewise provide crowd distraction. Turning Witness strength to zero does not remove those separate effects.

## Current inventory-opening penalty

Only an observed opening counts: the victim must detect and have LOS to the player, face within 70 degrees, not be sleeping or distracted by an active bump, and not be visibly headtracking someone else. The first observed opening adds 8 attention for 15 unpaused seconds. Another observed opening within that window adds 5 suspicion (capped at 60) and renews the 15-second window. Suspicion decays 0.15 attention per unpaused second. Default cap conversion is about 4 percentage points for the initial 8 attention and 2.5 additional points per repeat's 5 suspicion, before attention saturation, cap clamping, skill/stack/failure effects. This is not a direct flat subtraction from post-perk execution. Disabling the toggle clears opening windows and stops further opening suspicion; older general suspicion continues decaying.

## Optional equipped-item drop investigation (not shipped)

The intended default is disabled. Sleeping victims retain normal direct inventory transfer. Awake equipped clothing/armor and equipped weapons would become ground references at the victim's feet only after a successful legal pickpocket roll, preserving enchantment, tempering, unique IDs and NPC ownership. Other pocket contents stay on the existing path.

Native Actor::DropObject returns the dropped reference handle, TESObjectREFR::SetOwner supports owner assignment, and Actor::PickUpObject supports recovery. These APIs exist in the bundled CommonLib headers. Ownership must be an NPC/base or faction form accepted by ExtraOwnership, not a reference used as an invalid owner. The original victim reference must be tracked separately for per-NPC recovery and recognition.

The existing AttemptPickpocket hook performs the roll; the menu caller transfers the item afterward. Dropping a copy or moving it back out of the player's inventory would risk duplicate theft events, broken instance identity, and unwanted crime/XP. A scoped hook of the actual transfer is needed to redirect the original instance directly from the victim, then skip only that matching player transfer. This needs an independent SE/AE executable audit and gameplay validation.

Recovery would be deferred until the menu closes and use tracked drop handles. Out of combat, the victim should try to retrieve and equip the outfit only if alive, loaded, near it, the reference still exists and nobody else has taken it. In combat, clothing/armor recovery waits; a weapon can be considered. A forced PickUpObject call is not a demonstrated pathfinding/animation package, so a native AI package or minimal Papyrus quest/package bridge may be needed. No additional Papyrus is added in this release. Ownership should remain on the ground item so ordinary player pickup follows vanilla theft/bounty/aggro logic; no second synthetic alarm or fine should be added. Save/load, NPC death/unload, player/follower pickup, inaccessible drops, restoration mods and instance ownership need explicit tests before enabling this option.

## Validation boundary

Automated checks cover managed maximums, qualified/nonqualified bump limits, skill immunity, cooldowns and day-long failure blocking. MCM and native builds are compiled and packaged. Live perk acquisition/respec, bump recognition, witness behavior and co-save deadlines require in-game testing.
