# 0.1.34-dev recovery after a failed walk

Live log from 0.1.32: NPC 0001A684 dropped item 0001BE1A to FF000FF4; ground ownership matched expected 00013B9C. A recovery walk was dispatched at 16:14:31.624 and its Papyrus completion reported reached=false at 16:14:31.668. This proves the bridge ran; it does not prove the actor walked. The previous reached=false gate permanently prevented pickup or another walk. No Papyrus script log was available to explain why the native path failed.

Native pickup is now decided by current physical reach, not solely by the PathToReference Boolean. A same-cell loaded item within 120 units and 64 vertical units can be recovered after a short Havok obstruction ray passes. The horizontal ray is raised 32 units above both origins, uses the LOS collision layer and Havok world scale, and fails closed without a physics world. This avoids depending on actor-to-object LOS at the NPC's feet. No remote transfer/teleport is introduced. Sleeping/combat-clothing/scene/instance/ownership checks remain.

An item already within reach is picked up without first asking the actor to walk to the spot they already occupy. Farther/blocked items use the existing Papyrus path bridge. Failed walks can retry after five unpaused seconds, with a maximum of three failed walks per transient job. Pickup requests are throttled to two seconds; completion still requires the exact instance in the owner's inventory before equipping and clearing recovery. Existing saved recoveries get fresh transient path state after load.

New pickup logs include distance, path completion/result and failure count. The existing path-finished and actual recovered/equip-requested logs distinguish request, failure and completion. Build/core regression, reach boundaries and source contracts pass. In-game walking failure fallback, ray filtering, actual native pickup/equip, walls, slopes, separate floors, death/combat/unload/load and player pickup still require runtime validation. This changes eligibility/retry logic; it does not establish that every inaccessible navmesh target becomes walkable.
