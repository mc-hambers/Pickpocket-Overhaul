# 0.1.29-dev: timer layout and visual witnesses

The MCM timer formula and five current-setting examples are real right-column options, not description HTML. The right-hand rows are created hidden and disabled, revealed by timer hover, and hidden on other option highlights. Values are batched through SetTextOptionValue and SetOptionFlags, without ForcePageReset during hover. Public SkyUI source checked: https://github.com/schlangster/skyui/blob/master/dist/Data/Scripts/Source/SKI_ConfigBase.psc . Hover descriptions contain explanatory prose only. Item recognition defaults to 1 day in native defaults/fallback, shipped INI, and MCM reset. Existing chosen settings and previously recorded item deadlines are not rewritten.

Witness visibility uses synchronous Actor::CalculateLOS to player and victim, rather than queued RequestLOS. Results are cached for at most 100 milliseconds of wall time, invalidated by position changes and game session. This bounds repeated ray work during menu previews while allowing refresh when AI is paused. An unobstructed native eye/head/torso ray and the forward view are both required. General detection/hearing is not the eligibility gate.

The view cone is 110 degrees either side of the look direction. Headtracked targets recenter the view, rather than body direction alone excluding an NPC visibly looking at the theft. The player and victim must both be within that view. The 900-unit range remains. Candidates in adjacent exterior cells of the same worldspace now count; separate interiors/worldspaces do not. The unordered 64-candidate cutoff is removed, and repeated handles are deduplicated. Sleeping/follower/faction exclusions and existing witness weight/caps remain. Nearby-guard/alert awareness now also requires that qualifying visual check, preventing that separate penalty from acting through walls.

New 'Witness scan (direct native LOS)' log entries show eligible candidates, rejected view/range, occluded candidates, qualifying count, raw pressure, and contributing actor IDs when the scan changes. Use these with witness-strength and chance logs to distinguish detection issues from cap clamping, pressure, or faction exemptions. No universal witness-success claim is made from these tests.

Automated validation covers look-angle wrapping/headtrack recentering, peripheral/rear boundaries, blocked rays, sleeping, adjacent exterior cells versus isolated cells, and the new recognition default. Native/Papyrus builds and archive checks are separate from gameplay. Still needed in game: right-column reveal/hide and clipping, witness in front/behind/around corners, head turns, exterior cell borders, paused-menu updates, guild/follower exclusions, strength 0/1/3, and before/after debug cap values.
