# 0.1.42-dev repeat equipment recovery and crash review

Live evidence from 0.1.40: the same guard 00037080 dropped/recovered armor 0002150D at 16:47:27-30, then dropped/recovered it again at 16:48:41-16:49:44. Multiple pickups were already occurring. However, "recovered; equip requested" was only proof of issuing an equip call, not of wearing it. The pending record was immediately discarded, so a skipped/overridden queued equip had no follow-up.

The recovery is now retained after inventory pickup. Native queued forced equip is requested, then a later recovery pass checks the exact extra-data instance for ExtraWorn/ExtraWornLeft. Only confirmed wearing completes the record. Failed/overridden equips retry with throttling (2 seconds for initial attempts, then 10 seconds); every new drop resets its per-reference attempt state, including a reused world reference ID. Current scenes/sleep/clothing-in-combat defer equip. New logs distinguish "Recovery equip attempt ... awaiting worn confirmation" from "wearing confirmed". Native equip invocation remains on the game task thread; no immediate/applyNow equip is used.

Newest crash reviewed: 2026-10-19-16-50-22.log, Trainwreck 1.4, Skyrim 1.7.104. Fault SkyrimSE.exe+04D76C8, reading [RAX+14] with RAX=0. The probable stack is combat pathfinding (CombatPathDestinationRef/CombatPathingGoalPolicyFindTargetLocation), called through MaxsuCombatEscape::CellEventHandler::ProcessEvent at its source line 80, with LumaUtil's cell-change hook also present. PickpocketOverhaul.dll occurs in the loaded modules list, not the reported probable call stack. The report does not prove our plugin caused it, nor that Maxsu or Luma is independently at fault. The last overhaul log entry was an inventory opening on dynamic NPC FF000FBC, not a native equip call at the crash time.

Precautionary changes: no forced recovery walking during combat (nearby weapon pickup remains possible), no immediate marker deletion from a Papyrus callback or pre-load callback, and deferred marker retirement. Retired navigation goals wait at least 30 unpaused seconds and require their owner not be pathing/in combat; active unfinished recovery goals are protected. Reused markers are removed from the retirement queue. Pending retired and active marker IDs are carried in a separate SKSE record so saved navigation goals can be cleaned after loading; older records remain readable. These reduce lifecycle hazards but are not proof the combat-path crash is fixed.

Automated native/Papyrus/package and source contract checks pass. Required live checks: repeated same-item theft/pickup/equip (including actual worn flags), multiple simultaneous pieces, scene/combat transitions, cell change with MaxsuCombatEscape/LumaUtil, save/load during recovery, delayed goal cleanup and recurrence of the reported crash. Do not disable or modify companion mods as part of this patch without separate user direction.
